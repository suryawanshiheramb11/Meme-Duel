HOW TO PLAY

Type fast. Win two rounds.

The loop

  • Type the phrase on the paper strip. Every correct letter throws a strike; every 4th hit in a row is a finisher.
  • Finish a word for a bonus hit. Finish the whole phrase to play its meme and fire its special move.
  • A typo breaks your combo and can invite a counter-attack.
  • A match is first to two rounds. Win the last one by KO and you get 6 seconds to finish them: ↓ ↓ → ← then Shift.

Beating the bot

  • Every attack is telegraphed. HEAVY! is slow: Tab away. JUMP! is a low sweep: jump it.
  • A red ring with DODGE! is an unblockable ultra: Tab it, time a jump, or hit the bot to interrupt it.
  • Hard bots learn: spam one move and they start reading it. Hide behind guard and they grab you.
  • There are secret moves. Nothing here will tell you how.
